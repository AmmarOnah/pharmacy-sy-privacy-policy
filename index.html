Privacy Policy for Pharmacy SY

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pharmacy SY ("the App"), developed by Ammar Onah, handles information when you use the application. Pharmacy SY helps users find pharmacies on duty in Syrian cities and view their locations on a map.

Important: Pharmacy SY uses your device location only when you choose to use the map functionality and grant the required location permissions. The App does not require you to create an account.

1. Information We Access and Use

Location Information

Pharmacy SY requests Android's approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) and precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) permissions.

Location access is used only in the map screen. After you have granted the required location permissions and location services are enabled, the App obtains your current device location once and uses it to place your current position on the map together with pharmacies on duty.

The App does not continuously track your location, does not request background location access, and does not intentionally store your precise or approximate location on Pharmacy SY's own servers.

If you deny location permission or do not enable the device's location service, the location-based map feature may not be available. The App may check the permission and location-service status again when you return from Android settings so that it can determine whether it may obtain your location for the map.

Pharmacy Information

When you select a Syrian city, Pharmacy SY retrieves a JSON data file from an internet server containing information about pharmacies on duty in that city. The information may include pharmacy names, addresses, telephone numbers, coordinates, and other information necessary to display the pharmacies.

Users can filter the pharmacy list by district, view pharmacies on the map, call a pharmacy using its listed telephone number, and request directions through the Google Maps application.

Advertising and Device Information

Pharmacy SY uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements, including ads that may be displayed during navigation or interaction with buttons in the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from your device and your interactions with the App, including information such as IP address, advertising or app-related identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes.

The exact information processed by Google may depend on your device, advertising settings, the version and configuration of the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and applicable Google services.

2. How We Use Information

  • To show your current position on the map when the required location permissions are granted.
  • To display pharmacies on duty for the Syrian city selected by the user.
  • To provide pharmacy filtering by district.
  • To display pharmacy locations on the map.
  • To provide calling and directions features using the information displayed by the App.
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App where applicable.

3. How Information Is Shared

Pharmacy SY does not sell your personal information.

Pharmacy SY uses third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to provide advertising. Information processed by AdMob may be collected or shared by Google according to Google's policies and the applicable advertising configuration.

The App communicates with an internet server to retrieve the selected city's pharmacy JSON data. The App does not intentionally send your device location to that pharmacy-data server as part of the normal pharmacy-list request.

When you use the phone or directions features, the App may launch the relevant phone functionality or Google Maps application using the pharmacy telephone number or location information selected by you. Those external applications and services are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Location Permissions

Pharmacy SY requests location permission only for the map feature. The App requests and uses location in the map screen when the required permissions have been granted. Both approximate and precise location permissions may be requested by Android, and the App uses the available location only when the requirements for the map feature are satisfied.

You can deny, change, or revoke location permission at any time through Android settings. If permission is not granted, you can continue to use other parts of the App, but your current location cannot be displayed on the map.

5. Advertising Choices

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and advertising settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage available advertising and privacy controls through your Android device and Google account settings where those controls are provided.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Network communications used by the App should use secure HTTPS connections where supported by the relevant service.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Pharmacy SY does not require an account and does not maintain a user account containing personal profile information.

Pharmacy SY does not intentionally store the user's device location on its own servers. The location obtained for the map is used for the map feature and is not intended to be retained by Pharmacy SY after that use.

Information that may be automatically processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained according to those providers' policies, settings, and applicable requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Pharmacy SY is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or accounts.

9. Third-Party Services

Pharmacy SY may interact with third-party applications and services, including Google AdMob and Google Maps. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
